# Kukoo — Marketing Playbook

> Brand: **Kukoo** — run a real trademark/App Store search before launch (see main README). The competitor referred to as "Wakio" below is the actual third-party app of that name.

---

## 1. Competitive landscape

### Direct competitors (mission-based alarms)

| App | Position | Price signal | What they do well | Where they're weak |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Alarmy** ("Sleep If U Can") | Category king. ~$600k/mo revenue, 190k+ App Store ratings, 140M+ TikTok posts about it | Freemium + subscription | Massive UGC flywheel: floods TikTok with "my alarm made me do X" clips; hires micro-creators at $1–2 CPM and promotes the best clips as paid ads | Bloated app (10+ years of features), dated design, freemium means most users never pay |
| **Wayk** | Fast-follower clone, aggressive paid UA | Hard paywall, weekly anchor | Polished onboarding→paywall funnel (featured on funnel-teardown sites) | No brand, pure funnel play |
| **Wakz / Awyke / Wakey / Wak** | Clone swarm riding the same trend | Hard paywall | Speed to market | Interchangeable; none owns a distinctive mission or aesthetic |
| **Original Wakio** | Small indie, 157 ratings, 4.8★ | Hard paywall ($5.99/wk anchor) | Clean single-purpose scope | Tiny distribution |

### Adjacent competitors (compete for the same "fix my mornings" money)

- **Rise / Sleep Cycle / Pillow** — sleep science angle; they explain sleep, we *end* it. Don't fight them on data; fight on the moment of getting up.
- **Duolingo / Fabulous / Habit trackers** — same buyer (self-improvement subscriber), same streak psychology. Their playbook (streaks, guilt-tinged mascot energy, personalized plans) is proven on our exact audience.
- **Opal / one sec / ScreenZen** — "app that stops your bad habit" framing. Same purchase logic: *pay to be forced*.

### The gap Kukoo owns
Alarmy wins on distribution, not product love — its content is user-made chaos. Nobody in the clone swarm owns **"verified mornings"** as a brand idea: proof-of-wake (photo/motion, checked on-device, nothing uploaded) + streaks as a score. Position: **"The alarm that checks."** Privacy (no account, no servers) is a real differentiator vs. Alarmy and worth one line everywhere, not the headline.

## 2. Target audience

### Primary: "The Chronic Snoozer in a Self-Improvement Era" — 18–28
- Students + first/second-job professionals; phone charges within arm's reach; 3+ alarms set every night, all snoozed.
- Already pays for 1–2 self-improvement subs (Duolingo, gym app, Calm-ish). Vocabulary: "romanticize your mornings," "5–9 before my 9–5," "being *that girl*," "non-negotiables."
- Found on: TikTok + Reels (discovery), YouTube Shorts (consideration).
- Buying trigger: shame-adjacent humor → hope. They don't buy alarm features; they buy the *identity* of a person who gets up.
- Skews: TikTok discovery slightly female; "discipline/Monk-mode" angle skews male. Run both content lanes.

### Secondary: "The ADHD / executive-dysfunction morning" — 20–35
- Self-identified ADHD or "time-blind" audience; snooze isn't laziness, it's a genuine switch-cost problem. External forcing functions are a known, loved category here ("body doubling," "app blockers").
- Angle: *"It's not discipline, it's design."* Missions = built-in activation energy.
- Found on: ADHDtok, r/ADHD, r/getdisciplined.

### Tertiary: "Parents & shift-workers" — 28–45
- Must-not-oversleep stakes (kids' school, shifts, flights). Lower content virality, higher willingness to pay, great for App Store search ("loud alarm," "heavy sleeper").

### Anti-audience (don't chase)
Sleep-quality nerds (wearables crowd) and people who wake naturally — they churn instantly.

## 3. App Store screenshot plan (10 screens)

Format: portrait 6.9" + 6.5". Device frame on brand-dark background (#0D0F15), big headline top, electric-yellow→amber gradient (#FFD60A→#FFA000) on one keyword per headline. First 3 must work with zero context — most installs decide there.

| # | Screen shown | Headline | Subline |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ringing screen (pulsing orb, 6:00, "Start mission") | **Snooze is cancelled.** | The alarm that won't stop until you're actually up. |
| 2 | Mission picker sheet (all 6 missions) | **Prove you're awake.** | Photo, squats, or made bed — you choose your morning tax. |
| 3 | Sky Photo mission (camera pointing at dawn sky) | **Go outside. Yes, now.** | The alarm only stops when the sky is in frame. |
| 4 | Squats mission (rep ring at 7/10) | **10 squats or it keeps ringing.** | Motion-verified. Shaking doesn't count. |
| 5 | Object Hunt registration (coffee machine) | **Your coffee machine is the off button.** | Register an object across the house. Walk there to silence it. |
| 6 | Streak screen (flame, 23-day streak, calendar) | **Winning mornings, on a scoreboard.** | Streaks, best runs, and your real average wake time. |
| 7 | Sound picker (record your own) | **Wake up to your own voice.** | Or your mom's. Import any audio or video as your alarm. |
| 8 | Plan reveal chart (30-day curve) | **A plan built from your answers.** | Tell Kukoo how you snooze — it builds your wake-up curve. |
| 9 | Wake success screen (flame celebration) | **You're up. Mission complete.** | The best notification you'll see all day. |
| 10 | Privacy card (photos deleted, no account) | **Checked on your phone. Deleted after.** | No account. No servers. No photos of your bedhead anywhere. |

## 4. Ten viral video ideas (15–20s, AI-replicable, Higgsfield-ready)

Format rules learned from Alarmy's engine: post volume > polish, first frame = phone screen or a face reacting, on-screen captions always, sound-on POV humor. Each idea below = hook (first 1.5s), beats, and an AI generation note. For Higgsfield: generate the human shots as image→video (consistent character), screen-record the real app for UI inserts, cut in CapCut.

1. **"My alarm made me do squats at 6AM" (the category classic — proven format)**
   - Hook (text on screen): "my alarm won't turn off unless I do 10 squats"
   - Beats: dark room → alarm blaring → groggy person stands, phone in hand, does 3 exaggerated sleepy squats → cut to rep counter UI hitting 10 → alarm stops → face of despair/pride.
   - AI: Higgsfield character "sleepy person in dark bedroom, phone in hand, doing squats, handheld POV feel"; insert real screen recording of the squat counter.

2. **"POV: your alarm needs photographic evidence"**
   - Hook: "my alarm demanded a photo of the SKY"
   - Beats: alarm rings → person stumbles to balcony in blanket → holds phone up at grey dawn sky → shutter → "MISSION COMPLETE ✅" UI → deadpan caption: "I have now seen a sunrise against my will."

3. **The pain stat drop (data hook)**
   - Hook: giant text "you will snooze away 82 HOURS this year"
   - Beats: 3 fast clips of a person snoozing at different times/outfits (same bed) → counter ticking up → "that's 2 work weeks. asleep. badly." → app plan-reveal chart → CTA.
   - AI: fully generatable — same character, three micro-scenes + motion-graphics counter.

4. **"Rating my alarm's missions like a food critic"**
   - Hook: "ranking the ways my alarm torments me"
   - Beats: rapid-fire 5 missions with 2-word verdicts ("Sky photo: humbling. Squats: personal. Made bed: adult. Object hunt: cardio??") → freeze on streak screen.

5. **"My toxic relationship with snooze" (voiceover skit)**
   - Hook: "me and the snooze button were together for 9 years"
   - Beats: breakup-story voiceover over shots of the phone being put face-down, then the mission alarm "third-wheeling" → ends "we don't talk anymore. I'm up at 6:30 now."

6. **Couple/roommate cam (reaction format — Alarmy's best-performing genre)**
   - Hook: "I set my boyfriend's alarm to require 15 squats"
   - Beats: static "security cam" corner angle → alarm fires → confused half-asleep squats → partner filming shakes with laughter → caption "he doesn't know how to change it."

7. **"Apps I deleted vs the one that stayed" (listicle)**
   - Hook: "self-improvement apps I deleted in 2026"
   - Beats: fast icon flicks with one-word reasons ("guilt," "boring," "$$$") → "the one that stayed:" → 3-beat Kukoo montage (ring → sky photo → streak 23).

8. **The streak flex (aspirational lane, "that girl" aesthetic)**
   - Hook: "day 30 of my alarm making me touch grass"
   - Beats: aesthetic 5-shot morning montage (sky photo mission as shot #1, coffee, journal, gym fit) → streak screen 30 → "the app did what discipline couldn't."

9. **"I tried to outsmart my alarm" (fail compilation)**
   - Hook: "testing if my alarm can be cheated"
   - Beats: photo of ceiling → ❌ "that's not sky" → photo of a green towel → ❌ "that's not grass" → shaking phone wildly → counter stays at 0 → defeated real squats → ✅. Caption: "it knows."

10. **The 5:59→6:00 cinematic (brand-feel asset, run as the paid ad)**
    - Hook: silent black screen, clock 5:59 ticking
    - Beats: 6:00 flips → orb pulses → one unbroken slow push-in as person rises, opens curtains to sunrise, phone raised for sky photo → "MISSION COMPLETE" → logo + "Snooze is cancelled."
    - AI: this is the most Higgsfield-suited — one continuous cinematic shot, no dialogue.

### Distribution plan (copy Alarmy's engine, smaller)
- 2–3 faceless/character accounts posting 1–2×/day, rotating formats 1, 3, 6, 9 (the reaction + data hooks travel best).
- Reply-bait captions: "what mission would break you?" / "tag someone who snoozes 6 times."
- Whitelist the top organic clip each week as Spark Ads; kill anything under 3% CTR.
- Ideas 8 and 10 are the "brand" lane — lower volume, higher polish, used for the App Store preview video and paid retargeting.
