# App Review notes

Bangunin is a user-scheduled alarm-clock app. To test quickly:

1. Allow notifications during onboarding.
2. Create an alarm for two minutes in the future.
3. Choose a mission. Photo and exercise missions ask for camera permission only when used; custom recording asks for microphone permission.
4. Lock the iPhone and wait for the alarm notification.
5. Open the notification and complete the selected mission.

Important iOS behavior: Bangunin uses scheduled, time-sensitive local notifications. iOS controls notification presentation, sound, Focus/Silent behavior, and whether the app opens. The app does not claim to override those controls.
