# Closed-test plan

New personal Play developer accounts created after November 13, 2023 currently need at least 12 opted-in testers for 14 continuous days before applying for production access.

## Tester group

- Recruit at least 15 reliable testers so three can drop out without resetting the minimum.
- Each tester needs a Gmail or Google Workspace address.
- Keep every tester opted in for the full 14 days; ask them not to leave the test.
- Include several Android brands and OS versions, not only Pixel devices.

## Required scenarios

1. Create, edit, enable, disable, and delete an alarm.
2. Verify alarm delivery with the phone unlocked, locked, and after reboot.
3. Deny then enable notifications, exact alarms, full-screen intent, camera, and microphone permissions.
4. Complete Sky, Grass, Make Bed, and Object Hunt missions.
5. Complete camera-verified squats and normal two-handed push-ups in different lighting.
6. Abandon a mission and confirm the alarm returns to full volume.
7. Use all allowed snoozes and confirm the limit cannot be bypassed.
8. Record/import a custom sound and delete the alarm afterward.
9. Test Indonesian and English interfaces.
10. Purchase each subscription as a license tester; cancel and restore it.

## Feedback evidence for production-access application

Record tester count, device/Android version, dates active, tested scenarios, bugs reported, fixes shipped, and what changed because of feedback. Google asks about tester engagement and production readiness when the 14-day period ends.
