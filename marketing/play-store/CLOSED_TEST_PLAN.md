# Closed test and reviewer plan

## Reviewer instructions

1. Install from the assigned Play test track and allow notifications.
2. Create an alarm two minutes ahead and select a simple photo mission.
3. Lock the phone and wait. The alarm should present a full-screen alarm where Android permits; otherwise open its persistent high-priority notification.
4. Tap **Start mission**, allow the camera, complete the prompt, and confirm alarm audio/vibration end.
5. For Squats or Pushups, place the phone securely so the full body is visible. Camera frames and pose landmarks stay on-device and are not saved.
6. Premium products use Google Play Billing. Use the review/license-test account; there is no separate app login.

## Test matrix

Record device/Android version, expected result, actual result, and evidence for: fresh install; onboarding; notification allowed/denied; exact alarm allowed/denied; full-screen locked; swipe-away; force-stop (document Android limitation); reboot; silent/vibrate; DND; battery saver/optimization; multiple/repeating alarms; timezone/DST change; app update with scheduled alarms; low battery; offline mode; large text/screen reader; every mission and failed attempt; camera/microphone denial; purchase/trial/cancel/renew/restore; and notification-clear attempts.

For personal accounts created after 13 November 2023, keep at least 12 testers continuously opted in for 14 days, then apply from **Dashboard > Apply for production**. Testers should use alarms repeatedly and provide feedback through Play private feedback or `hello@bangunin.app`.
