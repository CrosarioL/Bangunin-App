# Launch and monetization plan

Use a 5% → 20% → 50% → 100% staged rollout, holding each stage at least 24–48 hours. Pause if crash-free users fall materially, ANR exceeds Play's bad-behavior threshold, exact/full-screen alarms regress, billing errors rise, or support reports missed alarms.

Privacy-preserving funnel metrics can initially be measured from Play Console aggregates plus voluntary tester feedback: listing-to-install, onboarding completion, first alarm creation, first successful mission, trial starts, trial-to-paid, monthly/yearly mix, cancellations/refunds, crashes/ANRs, and reviews. Do not add an analytics SDK without explicit approval.

Respond to reviews within two business days; never request sensitive mission data. Handle privacy requests at `hello@bangunin.app`. Use Indonesian launch messages focused on clear value, not fear or false urgency. Do not use countdowns, preselected consent, obstructed cancellation, or misleading “free” claims.

Illustrative gross estimate only: monthly Rp49,000 and yearly Rp199,000. If Google's service fee were 15%, approximate proceeds before tax/refunds would be Rp41,650 monthly or Rp169,150 yearly per paid subscriber. Confirm the actual service fee, withholding, VAT, payout currency, and Indonesian tax obligations in the account with Google and a qualified accountant.
